Privacy Policy

Applies to "roblox-factory" (YouTube uploader) and "Dotae Dev Uploader" (TikTok uploader) by Dotae Dev (도태개발자). Last updated: 2026-11-14.

What these tools are

Both are personal desktop tools that run only on the developer's own computer. They upload promotional videos of the developer's own Roblox games to the developer's own YouTube channel and TikTok account. They are not published, sold, or offered to any other user.

Information accessed

  • YouTube (Google API): permission to upload videos and set video details (title, description, tags, thumbnail) on the developer's channel, and to read basic channel information to confirm the target channel.
  • TikTok (Content Posting API): permission to send videos to the developer's TikTok inbox as drafts (video.upload), and basic profile information required by TikTok Login Kit.

How information is stored and used

  • Access tokens are stored only in a local file on the developer's computer. They are never sent to any server other than Google's or TikTok's own APIs.
  • No personal data of any other person is collected, stored, sold, or shared. No analytics, advertising, or cookies are used by these tools.
  • The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions or from TikTok's Settings ⟩ Security ⟩ Apps and services, and by deleting the local token files.
