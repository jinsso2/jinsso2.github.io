  1. 이슬 상품은 구매한 날부터 7일 안에, 받은 이슬을 사용하지 않았다면 청약철회를 할 수 있습니다.

    • 시작 꾸러미, 정원사 꾸러미, 봄 꾸러미: 구매 즉시 이슬·부스트·꾸미기·효과 지급
    • 바람 정기권: 구매 즉시 기간 시작
    • 후원: 구매 즉시 감사 선물 지급

Effective date: November 20, 2026 · Developer: DUMUNG · Privacy Policy

1. Paid items

The app sells the following items through Google Play billing: Dew (150, 1,000), No Ads, Permanent Fluff ×2, Starter Pack, Gardener Bundle, Spring Bundle, Garden Friend, Wind Pass (30 days) and Tip. Prices are shown on the purchase screen (Google Play). Permanent items (No Ads, Permanent ×2, the three bundles and the Garden Friend) are restored when you reinstall with the same Google account. Dew, the Wind Pass and tips are stored on the device and cannot be restored after uninstalling or changing devices.

2. Withdrawal

  • Dew packs can be withdrawn within 7 days of purchase if the Dew has not been used.
  • No Ads, Permanent ×2, the Garden Friend, the bundles, the Wind Pass and tips take effect immediately when purchased, so withdrawal is restricted. This is shown before purchase.
  • Free Dew (daily rewards, quests, ads) is spent first. Once any purchased Dew has been used, that purchase can no longer be withdrawn. You can check your paid Dew and each purchase in Settings › Purchase History.
  • If an item differs from its description, you can withdraw within 3 months of receiving it, or within 30 days of when you knew or could have known.

3. How to request a refund

Use Settings › Purchase History › Contact in the app, or email the address below with your Google Play order number (it starts with GPA. and is in your Google Play receipt). You can also request a refund through Google Play. If the purchase can be withdrawn, the developer cancels the payment through Google Play within 3 business days. Refunded or cancelled purchases, including refunds made directly through Google Play, are removed from the app: granted Dew is deducted (from future Dew if you do not have enough), and item effects are removed.

4. Purchases by minors

If a minor made a purchase without a legal guardian's consent, the minor or the guardian can cancel it. The developer may ask for proof of guardianship.
